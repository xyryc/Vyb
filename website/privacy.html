1. Introduction

Welcome to Vyb, an offline-first local audio player application designed and developed by Stonewell Studio ("we," "us," or "our"). Vyb provides users with high-fidelity local music playback, custom playlist organization, spatial sound adjustments, and playback control features on the Android operating system.

We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it. This Privacy Policy outlines our principles regarding data collection, device permissions, and local security. By installing and using Vyb, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

Core Privacy Principle: Vyb is an offline-first application. We do not run background telemetry that uploads your personal audio tracks, playlists, or listening history to external web servers. Your data belongs strictly on your physical device.

2. App Permissions & Security Policy

To deliver a seamless, high-fidelity audio experience, Vyb requires specific system permissions on Android devices (Target SDK 36 / Android 14+ compatible). We use system framework APIs to minimize permission footprint:

Required

Storage Access via SAF

Vyb uses Android's native Storage Access Framework (SAF) document pickers. This allows you to import specific MP3 files or music folders without granting broad device-wide storage access permissions.

Required

Foreground Media Playback

Declared via FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PLAYBACK. Required by Android 14+ to run the audio playback engine uninterrupted when Vyb is in the background or screen is turned off.

Required

Notification Permission

Uses POST_NOTIFICATIONS on Android 13+. Allows Vyb to display compact media player controls (Play, Pause, Skip, Like) in your system notification shade and lock screen.

Optional

System Overlay (Draw Over Apps)

Enables Vyb's optional Floating Dynamic Island overlay so you can monitor song playback and audio visualizer waveforms while using other applications.

3. Data & Metadata Processing

Vyb processes audio data and listening stats locally. Let's break down exactly what happens to your data:

  • Audio Metadata Extraction: When you import files, Vyb uses Android's native MediaMetadataRetriever to extract track names, artist names, album titles, durations, and album artwork. This data is stored locally in your SQLite Database.
  • Local Room Database: All user-defined database information (such as your playlists, liked tracks, equalizer bands, customized presets, and system options) is stored on your device using Android's **Room SQLite Library**.
  • Smart Listening Insights: Your listening statistics (total play time, top-played tracks, preferred genres) are parsed dynamically from local databases on your device. We have no remote API access to these statistics.

4. Local Security & Storage

Vyb secures your audio files and metadata using Android system-level sandboxing technologies:

When files are imported, Vyb copies them into the application's isolated sandboxed directory. This ensures other apps cannot easily read, modify, or corrupt your imported audio files. When you delete a track or uninstall Vyb, all associated sandbox files and metadata in the Room database are immediately cleared by the Android OS.

5. Third-party Services & HTTPS Security

Vyb processes audio playback completely offline. To enhance the experience, Vyb optionally fetches high-resolution album artwork and lyrics using secure external public APIs:

  • iTunes Search API: Anonymous GET requests over HTTPS to fetch official high-resolution album artwork.
  • LRCLIB API: Anonymous GET requests over HTTPS to fetch synchronized audio lyrics.
Network Security Guarantee: Vyb strictly enforces encrypted HTTPS transport across all network calls. Unencrypted cleartext HTTP traffic is completely disabled app-wide in compliance with Google Play Security Policies.

6. Google Play Data Safety Declaration

For complete transparency with Google Play Store users, below is our formal Data Safety breakdown:

No Data Collected

User Data Collection

Vyb does NOT collect, harvest, store, or transmit any Personally Identifiable Information (PII), audio files, or user contact details to external servers.

No Data Shared

Third-party Data Sharing

No user data or audio usage analytics are shared with third parties, ad networks, or data brokers.

Encrypted Transport

Security Practices

All transient metadata queries (artwork/lyrics) use end-to-end HTTPS encryption. App storage is fully isolated within Android's app sandbox.

Instant Erasure

Data Deletion Mechanism

Deleting a track or uninstalling Vyb instantly and permanently erases all associated sandbox databases, artwork caches, and metadata from your device.

6. Children's Privacy

Vyb is fully compliant with children's safety and privacy directives. Because we do not collect, request, parse, or transmit any personally identifiable information (PII) to remote locations, Vyb is safe and suitable for users of all ages, including children under the age of 13.
