Vyb Privacy Policy

Your music, your data. Kept completely local, secure, and private on your Android device.

1. Introduction

Welcome to Vyb, an offline-first local audio player application designed and developed by Stonewell Studio ("we," "us," or "our"). Vyb provides users with high-fidelity local music playback, custom playlist organization, spatial sound adjustments, direct URL audio downloads, and playback control features on the Android operating system.

We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it. This Privacy Policy outlines our principles regarding data collection, device permissions, and network practices. By installing and using Vyb, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

Core Privacy Principle Vyb is an offline-first application. We do not run background telemetry that uploads your personal audio tracks, playlists, URLs, or listening history to external web servers. Your data belongs strictly on your physical device.

2. App Permissions & Security Policy

Vyb supports Android 7.0 and above. We use native system framework APIs to minimize permission footprint:

Required

Storage Access (SAF)

Uses Android's native Storage Access Framework document pickers to import specific MP3 files or folders without requesting broad device-wide storage permissions.

Required

Foreground Media Playback

Declared via FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PLAYBACK to allow playback to continue smoothly when Vyb is minimized or the screen is locked.

Required

Notification Controls

Uses POST_NOTIFICATIONS to display playback controls in the system notification drawer and lock screen.

Network

Internet Access

Uses INTERNET strictly for user-initiated actions: fetching optional cover art and downloading audio files when you explicitly trigger the URL Import feature.

3. Data & Metadata Processing

All audio data processing and analytics happen locally on your hardware:

  • Audio Metadata Extraction: Reads track names, artist names, album titles, and embedded artwork into your local SQLite Room database.
  • Local Room Database: Playlists, liked tracks, equalizer bands, and settings persist solely on your device's internal storage.
  • Smart Listening Insights: Listening metrics (total play time, top genres, play counts) are computed on-device with zero remote server logging.

4. Local Security & Storage Sandboxing

Imported audio files and downloaded media are safely isolated inside Vyb's protected application sandbox directory. Other third-party apps cannot access or tamper with these files. When you delete a track or uninstall the app, all sandbox files, cached artwork, and Room database records are permanently deleted by the operating system.

5. Network Access, Third-Party Hosts & Direct URL Imports

A. User-Initiated Network Access & URL Audio Import

Vyb includes an optional feature that allows users to download audio files directly to their device by entering a remote URL. When this feature is used:

  • Direct Client-to-Host Connection: Network connections are established directly between your Android device and the remote web host serving the audio file.
  • No Intermediary Proxy: Vyb does not operate an intermediary proxy server. Traffic flows directly to the destination server without passing through Stonewell Studio.
  • Zero Logging or Telemetry: We do not log, inspect, store, or transmit entered URLs, downloaded filenames, or IP addresses to any analytics or developer servers.
  • Local Sandboxing: Downloaded media is stored directly in your local app sandbox for offline playback.

B. Optional Album Artwork Lookup

When enabled in Settings, Vyb can optionally fetch high-resolution cover art for your local songs via Apple's iTunes Search API over encrypted HTTPS. Only the song's non-identifying track title and artist name are sent. You can disable this feature entirely in Settings.

Encrypted Transport All outbound network queries enforce strict HTTPS/TLS protocols. Unencrypted cleartext HTTP traffic is disabled app-wide in compliance with Google Play Security Policies.

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers regarding Vyb's privacy and offline architecture.

Does Vyb log or track the audio URLs I download?

No. When you download an audio file via a URL, the connection is direct between your device and the remote host. Vyb does not operate an intermediary server, does not log entered URLs, and collects zero analytics or identifiers.

Are my local MP3 audio files ever uploaded or sent anywhere?

Absolutely not. Vyb operates strictly locally. Your audio files are processed on-device to play music, extract ID3 tags, and generate waveforms. We do not operate servers to store your music files.

Why does Vyb keep playing music when the screen is locked?

Vyb uses Android's standard Foreground Service and MediaSession API to keep audio playing in the background, displaying controls in the notification drawer so you can control playback without keeping the app open.

What happens when I uninstall Vyb?

Since Vyb stores all track databases and settings in Android's local sandboxed app storage, uninstalling Vyb instantly clears all related data from your device.
