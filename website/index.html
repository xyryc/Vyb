Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about listening with Vyb.

Is Vyb completely free with no ads?

Yes, 100%. Vyb has zero advertisements, no premium subscriptions, and no paywalls. All sound features, equalizers, and visualizers are completely free to use.

How do I add music to Vyb?

You can import existing MP3 folders or files directly from your phone's storage. You can also download tracks into your library directly by entering an audio file URL.

Does Vyb work completely offline?

Yes! Vyb is built from the ground up as an offline player. Once audio is on your device, you can listen on flights, subway rides, or in remote areas with zero internet connection or data usage.

Do I need an account to use Vyb?

No account, email, or login required. Just install the app from Google Play, import your music, and start enjoying your songs.
